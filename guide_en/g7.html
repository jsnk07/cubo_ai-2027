Preparation

Heading to the Technical Bootcamp

One week to go deeper into agents, RAG, MCP, security and infrastructure, and 48 hours to prove it.

Bootcamp · 1The week

The Bootcamp is for those who passed the Challenge selection. There is no more filtering here: you go deeper. It is in person and intensive, full time.

  • Days 1 to 4: lectures, guided workshops and labs on Transformers and LLMs, agents and multi-agent orchestration, advanced RAG and vector databases, MCP servers, evaluation and observability, AI security, deployment, costs and sovereign AI.
  • Days 5 and 6: a 48-hour hackathon, then a 15-minute group pitch and the individual defense of your role.

Arrive with your environment ready and your F5 DIYs working: the hackathon leaves no time to install things.

Bootcamp · 2Project lines and roles

Each team chooses a project line:

  1. Autonomous agents with MCP integration for institutional or business automation.
  2. Multimodal RAG systems with traceability and verifiable citation.
  3. Observability, auditing and automated LLM evaluation.
  4. Sovereign AI on local infrastructure or open models.
  5. Specialized copilots for education, health, finance or public services.
  6. AI-driven cybersecurity: vulnerabilities and threat analysis.
  7. Interactive simulators and multi-agent workflows.

Within the team, each person takes main responsibility for a role, and is evaluated on that role:

AI and agent integrationModels, agentic flows, MCP servers, RAG pipelines and prompts.
Backend and infrastructureServer, business logic, vector and relational databases.
Interfaces and integrationsInternal APIs, third-party tools, integration testing and documentation.
UX, frontend and productInterface, human-AI interaction, operational validation and the pitch.

Bootcamp · 3Your hackathon environment

Before Day 5, check that you have working:

  • Docker with Ollama and at least one chat model and one embeddings model downloaded.
  • Your F5 MCP server responding in the MCP Inspector.
  • An evaluation harness you can reuse (from the Challenge starter kit).
  • A repository template with the standard structure, README and license.
  • GitHub access from your computer without typing a password every time.

If the venue's internet fails, a local model keeps working. That is a practical reason, besides the sovereign one, to master Ollama before the hackathon.

Bootcamp · 4How you will be evaluated

The final evaluation separates three questions: did you complete the activities?, did you demonstrate the competencies and authorship?, did you defend your work? Completing is not enough. Projects are scored on six dimensions:

  1. Technical implementation: model and agent integration, backend, interfaces and documentation.
  2. Cybersecurity and governance: data protection, permission isolation, prompt injection and human oversight.
  3. User experience: usability and clear interaction with the system.
  4. Problem solving: how well it delivers what it promised.
  5. Scalability and performance: latency, token and compute efficiency, stability.
  6. Presentation and individual defense: clarity of the pitch and defense of your decisions and your contribution.

Bootcamp · 5The 15-minute pitch

A structure that works:

MinutesContent
0–2The problem and who it affects, with a data point.
2–4The solution and why AI is the right tool.
4–9Live demo, with one case that works and one where the system says it doesn't know.
9–11Evidence: evaluation metrics, cost per query, risks and controls.
11–13What each member contributed, in one sentence each.
13–15Limitations and next steps.

Have a short backup video in case the model or the network fails live, and say so honestly if you use it.

Bootcamp · 6Your credential and how it is verified

If you pass the final evaluation, the National Artificial Intelligence Agency (ANIA) issues your institutional credential and publishes it on its official channel. In addition, the certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint is computed and timestamped with OpenTimestamps.

That way anyone, for example an employer, can check on their own that your certificate is authentic and has not been modified, without having to call anyone. The public record only shows the necessary data.

DIY WORKSHOP · BOOTCAMP

4-hour mini hackathon

Rehearse the hackathon on a small scale: one team, one problem, four hours and a five-minute pitch.

4 hoursTeam of 3 or 4Before the Bootcamp
Technical route
An agent that does one thing well
  1. Choose a project line and a very narrow case.
  2. Hour 1: architecture on one page and role split.
  3. Hours 2 and 3: build a new tool related to the case on top of your F5 MCP server.
  4. Add a basic defense against prompt injection: the agent ignores instructions that come inside documents.
  5. Hour 4: run 10 evaluation cases and prepare the demo.
Complementary route
Product, evidence and pitch
  1. Write the problem and the user in three lines.
  2. Design the human-AI interaction flow, with the point where a person approves.
  3. Prepare the 10 evaluation cases with their expected answer.
  4. Calculate the cost per query and the main risks.
  5. Build the five-minute pitch with the Bootcamp · 5 structure, in short form.
Deliverable

Repository with the minimum solution, results of 10 evaluation cases and a five-minute pitch recorded or presented to another team.

Checklist
  • Each person can explain their role in one minute.
  • The demo includes a case where the system says it doesn't know.
  • At least one security measure is implemented.
